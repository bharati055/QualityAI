`kind` is `incident`, `revert`, or `false_positive`.

Save as `.qualityai/evals/records/rec-replace-me.md`. Then write the case from `CASE-TEMPLATE.md`.

## What happened

What escaped, what was reverted, or which finding was wrong. One short paragraph a later reader can re-run from.

## Evidence

Pasted log, diff excerpt, revert reason, or the finding text. A ticket id may be pasted as text. Do not put secrets here. Do not call Jira or GitHub from the harness.
